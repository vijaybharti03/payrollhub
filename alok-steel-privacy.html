3. Information Collected & Processing Mechanisms

3.1 Local Business, Worker & Payroll Records (Device Storage Only)

Alok Steel Payroll allows application administrators and site supervisors to record operational data needed for payroll maintenance. This information includes:

  • Worker Master Profiles: Worker names, unique worker IDs, contact phone numbers, designation, and assigned daily/hourly wage rates.
  • Site Attendance Logs: Shift records, daily attendance status (Present, Absent, Half-Day, Overtime hours), and site location assignments.
  • Advance Payment Transactions: Money advance amounts given to workers, date stamps, payment modes, and digital signature acknowledgments.
  • Wage & Payslip Data: Calculated net pay, deductions, advance recoveries, and generated PDF payslips.

Note: All of the above data is stored locally inside an encrypted SQLite database on the Android device running the application. It is not transmitted to any remote central server owned by Alok Steel.

3.2 Mobile Device Runtime Permissions

To execute core payroll functions, Alok Steel Payroll requests specific runtime permissions on Android:

  • Storage / Media Files Access: Required strictly to export generated worker PDF payslips, Excel/CSV summary reports, and local database backup files to device storage.
  • Camera / Signature Capture: Used solely when capturing worker receipt photos, physical vouchers, or digital touch signatures for advance logs.
  • Internet Access: Used exclusively to facilitate voluntary user-initiated Google Drive synchronization and checking for mandatory security or app updates.

3.3 Google Drive Backup & OAuth Authentication

If you choose to enable Google Drive Cloud Backup in Alok Steel Payroll:

  • The Application connects directly to Google OAuth 2.0 services using the restricted drive.appdata permission scope.
  • The Application can only access its own private, isolated folder in your Google Drive. It has no access to your personal files or general Google Drive contents.
  • Authentication tokens are securely saved in Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences on your phone.
  • Backup files move directly between your mobile device and Google Drive via encrypted HTTPS/TLS 1.3 channels without passing through any intermediate server.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by Alok Steel Payroll is used strictly for operational payroll administration, including:

  • Calculating accurate daily worker wages, overtime pay, and net payouts.
  • Tracking advance balance deductions and maintaining transparent transaction histories.
  • Generating downloadable PDF payslips and CSV spreadsheet reports for site audits.
  • Enabling safe backup and restoration of your payroll data across devices via your personal Google Drive.
  • Providing technical customer support upon explicit user request.

5. Data Security & Storage Protection

We prioritize data protection through robust technical controls:

  • Android OS Application Sandboxing: Device data is stored in the application's isolated internal storage directory, inaccessible to other regular apps installed on the phone.
  • Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3): Any external communications (such as Google Drive backup sync) use mandatory TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Zero Remote Server Vulnerability: Because we do not store your worker or payroll records on central web databases, your information is protected against centralized cloud database leaks or server breaches.

6. User Data Rights & Deletion Control

As the owner of your data in Alok Steel Payroll, you maintain total control:

  • Data Export: You can export worker master files, attendance logs, and advance ledgers to CSV or PDF files at any time.
  • Device Data Deletion: Clearing data for Alok Steel Payroll under Android Settings > Apps, or uninstalling the application, permanently erases all local database records from your device.
  • Cloud Backup Deletion: You can delete backup files directly from your personal Google Drive account under "Manage Apps > Alok Steel Payroll".

7. Third-Party Sharing & Data Monetization Policy

Alok Steel Payroll operates under a zero-data-monetization policy:

  • We do NOT sell, rent, trade, lease, or share any personal, worker, financial, or attendance information with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.
  • We do NOT embed third-party advertising SDKs or unauthorized user tracking telemetry.
  • Information is shared only with standard infrastructure providers (Google Play Services / Google Drive API) solely for user-initiated cloud backup operations or legal requirements under applicable Indian law.

8. Children's Privacy Statement

Alok Steel Payroll is designed exclusively for business contractors, industrial maintenance engineers, and payroll managers. It is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
