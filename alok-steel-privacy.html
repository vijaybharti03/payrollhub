3. Information We Collect & Processing Mechanisms

3.1 Local Business, Worker & Payroll Data (Stored on Device)

Alok Steel Payroll enables business owners, site contractors, and payroll administrators to record operational data needed for daily site maintenance and worker payments. This information includes:

  • Worker Master Profiles: Worker names, unique IDs, contact phone numbers, designation, and daily wage rates.
  • Site Attendance Logs: Shift records, daily attendance status (Present, Absent, Half-Day, Overtime), and site location logs.
  • Advance Payment Transactions: Money advance amounts paid to workers, payment modes, receipt dates, and digital signatures.
  • Calculated Wage Ledgers: Net wage calculations, advance recoveries, and generated PDF payslips.

Crucial Data Privacy Note: All worker, attendance, and payroll records are stored locally inside an encrypted SQLite database on the Android device. Jay Internet Center does not host, upload, or process your worker financial data on external servers.

3.2 Mobile Permissions Requested

To execute core application features, Alok Steel Payroll requests the following Android runtime permissions:

  • Storage / Media Files Access: Required strictly to export generated worker PDF payslips, Excel/CSV summary reports, and local database backup files to your phone's storage.
  • Camera / Signature Access: Used exclusively when capturing worker digital signatures, physical vouchers, or payment receipt photos for advance logs.
  • Internet Access: Used solely to facilitate voluntary Google Drive synchronization and checking for mandatory security or app updates.

3.3 Google Drive Backup & OAuth Authentication Data

When you voluntarily enable Google Drive Auto-Sync in Alok Steel Payroll:

  • The Application connects directly to Google OAuth 2.0 services using the restricted drive.appdata scope.
  • The Application can only access its own private, isolated folder in your Google Drive. It cannot view or read your general Google Drive files.
  • OAuth tokens are saved in Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences on your mobile device.
  • Database backup files pass directly between your mobile phone and your personal Google Drive account via encrypted HTTPS/TLS 1.3 channels without touching any external server owned by Jay Internet Center.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed inside Alok Steel Payroll is utilized exclusively to:

  • Calculate daily worker wage totals, overtime bonuses, and net wage distributions.
  • Maintain transparent worker advance balances and recovery transaction logs.
  • Generate downloadable PDF payslips and CSV spreadsheet reports for site accounting.
  • Facilitate user-controlled database backup and restore across devices via personal Google Drive.
  • Provide direct developer support and resolve technical queries upon explicit user request.

5. Data Security & Technical Safeguards

We implement industry-standard technical security controls:

  • Android Device Isolation: Data stored in the local SQLite database is isolated by Android OS app sandboxing.
  • Encrypted Connections: Google Drive backup requests use mandatory TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Zero Server Storage: Since Jay Internet Center does not operate remote cloud database servers for worker records, your data is immune to centralized cloud data breaches.

6. Data Sharing & Third-Party Policy

Jay Internet Center and Alok Steel Payroll enforce a strict Zero Third-Party Data Sharing policy:

  • We do NOT sell, rent, lease, trade, or share any worker records, employee financial data, or business analytics to advertising networks or data brokers.
  • We do NOT embed third-party advertising SDKs or unauthorized tracking code in the app.
  • Information is shared only with essential infrastructure providers (Google Play Services / Google Drive API) solely for user-initiated cloud backup operations or legal requirements under applicable Indian law.

7. User Rights, Data Control & Deletion

As the owner of your data in Alok Steel Payroll:

  • Device Data Deletion: Clearing data for Alok Steel Payroll under Android Settings > Apps, or uninstalling the app, permanently removes all local databases from your mobile phone.
  • Google Drive Backup Deletion: You can delete backup files directly from your personal Google Drive account under "Manage Apps > Alok Steel Payroll".
  • Instant Data Export: You can export full worker databases and attendance ledgers to CSV or PDF files whenever needed.
