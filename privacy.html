3. Information We Collect

3.1 Local Business & Payroll Data (Stored on Device)

Payroll Hub allows you to enter worker records, site attendance, wage rates, advance payments, and digital signatures. All this information is stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database on your Android device.

3.2 Mobile Permissions Requested

To provide core functionality, the Payroll Hub Android application requests the following runtime permissions:

  • Storage / Media Access: Needed strictly to export PDF payslips, Excel/CSV reports, and backup files to your local device storage.
  • Camera / Signature Access: Used exclusively when capturing worker digital signatures or receipt photos for advance logs.
  • Internet Access: Used solely to facilitate voluntary Google Drive synchronization and checking for application updates.

3.3 Google Drive Backup & OAuth Data

When you enable Google Drive Auto-Sync within the app, Payroll Hub requests access to app-specific hidden storage (drive.appdata scope) in your own Google Drive.

  • We do not have access to your general Google Drive files.
  • Your Google account tokens are stored securely in Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences.
  • Your database backup files are uploaded directly from your device to your Google Drive account without passing through any server owned by Jay Internet Center.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use local and optional cloud backup information exclusively to:

  • Provide worker advance payment tracking, site attendance logs, and payroll calculation.
  • Generate downloadable PDF payslips and CSV reports.
  • Facilitate database backup and restore across your devices via Google Drive.
  • Provide customer support and resolve technical queries.

5. Data Security

We implement stringent technical and organizational security controls:

  • Device Isolation: Data stored in the Android SQLite database is protected by Android OS app sandboxing.
  • Encrypted Connections: Any sync requests with Google APIs use TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Zero Server Storage: Since Jay Internet Center does not operate remote cloud database servers for worker records, your data is immune to centralized server data breaches.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your data to advertising agencies, brokerages, or third parties.

We may interact with official service providers solely for essential functionality:

  • Google Play Services & Google Drive API: Used for optional user-driven cloud backups.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation in India.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because your data resides on your device and in your personal Google Drive account:

  • Device Data Deletion: Clearing the Payroll Hub app storage or uninstalling the app permanently removes all local databases from your phone.
  • Google Drive Backup Deletion: You can delete backup files directly from your Google Drive account at any time under "Manage Apps".
  • Data Export: You can export full worker databases and attendance logs as CSV files whenever needed.

8. Children's Privacy

Payroll Hub is designed for commercial business, contracting, and payroll management purposes and is not directed towards children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
