Frequently Asked Questions

Who develops and maintains Payroll Hub?

Payroll Hub is developed and operated by Jay Internet Center, a registered Micro Enterprise under the Ministry of MSME, Government of India (Udyam Reg. No: UDYAM-CG-13-0004087), located in Raigarh, Chhattisgarh.

How does the worker advance tracking work?

When you give cash or bank advance to a worker, select the worker in the app and tap "Add Advance". You can record amount, payment mode, notes, and optionally take a digital signature. The advance is automatically deducted from their end-of-month final payroll calculation.

Can I export data to Excel or CSV?

Yes! The app supports robust CSV and Excel export options for attendance registers, advance logs, and full monthly payroll summaries. You can also import existing worker lists via CSV.

What happens if I change my phone or uninstall the app?

With Google Drive Auto-Sync enabled, your database is backed up securely to your personal Google Drive. When installing the app on a new device, simply sign in with your Google account and restore your complete database in seconds.
