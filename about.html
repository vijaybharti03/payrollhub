Who We Are

A Registered Indian MSME Dedicated to Practical Software Solutions

Established on May 1, 2021, in Raigarh, Chhattisgarh, Jay Internet Center is a Govt. of India recognized Micro Enterprise (Udyam Reg. No: UDYAM-CG-13-0004087) specializing in Information Technology, computer consultancy, software development, and digital services.

We noticed a critical challenge faced by construction contractors, factory supervisors, and SMB managers across India: managing daily worker advances, attendance ledgers, and site transfers using physical notebooks led to lost records, wage disputes, and complex payroll calculations.

To solve this, we created Payroll Hub — an offline-first, fast, and easy-to-use Android application designed specifically for field managers and business owners.
